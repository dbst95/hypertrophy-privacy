Hypertrophy

A strength-training log for iPhone.

Hypertrophy keeps your workouts on this iPhone. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server. Questions, bugs, or feature requests go to a real person.

Support

Email strole95@gmail.com. You will get a reply.

Useful things to include if you are reporting a problem:

  • What you were doing when it happened
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The app version, shown in Settings → About

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. The short version: workouts stay on your iPhone. Health is optional and off until you turn it on.
