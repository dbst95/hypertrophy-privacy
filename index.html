Privacy Policy for Hypertrophy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Hypertrophy is a strength-training log for iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your iPhone. The app has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics, and it does not send your training log anywhere.

Information the app stores

Everything you enter is saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage:

  • Workouts you log, including their names, dates, exercises, sets, weights and repetitions.
  • Routines and any custom exercises you create.
  • Your settings, such as your preferred units, appearance, app icon, and rest timer length.

None of this is transmitted off your device. The developer cannot see it, and neither can anyone else.

Apple Health

Connecting Hypertrophy to Apple Health is optional, and off unless you turn it on in Settings and grant permission. iOS asks you separately for each type of data, and you can change or withdraw permission at any time in the Health app or in Settings on your iPhone.

When it is turned on, the app:

  • Reads your bodyweight, so bodyweight exercises such as pull-ups and dips count correctly toward your training volume. It uses the most recent weigh-in on or before the workout's date, not today's weight.
  • Reads your heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate and sleeping wrist temperature, which are used to estimate how recovered you are and shown on the Readiness screen. That readout is training status, not a medical diagnosis.
  • Writes your finished workouts to Apple Health, so they appear alongside your other activity.

Health data is read onto your device, used to produce what you see in the app, and nothing more. It is never transmitted off your device, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold or shared with anyone.

Notifications and lock screen

If you use the rest timer, the app asks permission to send you a notification when your rest is over. These notifications are created and delivered by your iPhone itself. The Live Activity that shows your workout on the lock screen works the same way — it is displayed by your device, not sent from anywhere.

Backups

Export and restore copy your log as a file you choose. Hypertrophy does not upload that file. Where it goes after you export — Files, Mail, AirDrop, or another app — is your choice, and that destination's own privacy policy applies.

Information the app does not handle

  • There are no accounts, logins, or passwords.
  • There is no analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party code.
  • Nothing is uploaded to any server, because the app has none.
  • No information is sold or shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete any workout inside the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything it has stored. Workouts that were written to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there.

Children

Hypertrophy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Since the app collects nothing at all, no such information is ever received.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, it will be updated here, and the date at the top will change.
